Club signup is free

There is no fee for a facility to join the tour. You are not charged anything, at any point, for listing your club or fielding players.

Send your facility details through the club application and you will be added to the facility map and the club roster. What to have ready: facility name, contact name, email, phone, city and state, your platform (SGT/GSPro or Trackman), and bay count.

Whatever you charge your own players for bay time or as a club registration fee is entirely up to you. That money goes to your club directly and never passes through the tour.
