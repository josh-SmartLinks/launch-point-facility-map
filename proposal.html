LaunchPoint Global Tour: Initial Proposal

Everything below is a first draft. It is 100% open to feedback and redlining. Nothing here is locked in. Bring changes to the August 15 meeting or send them ahead of time.

Three seasons: Fall Tour, Winter Tour, Spring Tour.

Format

  • Each club selects the courses they want to play, including local courses near them
  • Selections are compiled into a schedule and agreed upon by all clubs before the season starts
  • One course per week
  • Each club gets to pick their favorite course to play. If there are more clubs than weeks in the tour, we’ll run a popularity vote to narrow the list.
  • GS Pro on one side, Trackman on the other

Competition Rules

  • Sim settings are locked and identical across all clubs
  • Gross scoring only, open to discussion
  • One scored attempt per week. Round must be declared before it starts and streamed start to finish. That score stands. Unlimited unscored practice rounds allowed.
  • Weekly window runs Monday through Sunday, open to discussion
  • Leaderboard updated live on this site
  • Tiebreaker rules to be discussed

Pricing

  • Everything is funded per enrolled player, not per club. A club with 6 players pays for 6. A club with 40 pays for 40. Bay count and club size never penalize anyone.
  • Software cost is split by total enrolled player count across all clubs, not evenly across clubs
  • One entry fee per player, collected at the start of the tour. That fee covers the player’s software cost share plus their prize pool contribution.
  • Full breakdown of what covers software versus what goes to the pot is published to every club
  • All software costs are at cost. Zero collection on my end. Every number fully disclosed.
  • Trackman pricing in progress
  • Option to bundle all three tours for players who want the full season

Prize Pool

  • Prize pool is 100% per player funded, so the pot grows with every entry. More players, bigger payout. No fixed pot, no club subsidizing another.
  • Payouts are spread across the field, not just the top. Proposed paying positions: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, then 10th, 25th, 50th, and 100th, scaling with field size.
  • All paying positions are published before the tour starts. Nothing is randomly drawn.
  • Winners per club, winners per course, and potentially a season-long overall champion
  • Weekly closest to the pin and low round of the week can run alongside the main format
  • Prize packages could include cash or a trip to a premium club (Pebble Beach as an example)
  • Cash prize tax review in progress

Money Handling

  • Zero profit on my end. I take nothing. All software costs go directly to SGT or Trackman. I collect nothing from any club or player beyond that actual cost. Every number published.
  • Prize money is held in a separate escrow or dedicated holding account, not by me and not by any individual club. Ledger visible to every participating club.
  • Each club remits its enrolled player fees in full before week one. No rolling collection once the tour starts.
  • Enrollment locks at the remittance deadline. Players added after that go to the next tour.
  • If a club drops mid-tour, its enrolled players stay eligible and finish the season. Funds already remitted stay in the pot. No other club is on the hook for a shortfall.
  • If a player drops mid-tour, their contribution stays in the pot. No refunds once play begins.
  • Payouts issue from escrow after the final week and after winning rounds are reviewed.

Sponsorship

  • Actively exploring sponsors for the tour. More details to come.

Integrity

  • All scored rounds must be livestreamed
  • Streams stored on YouTube and linked to each round. Can remain private, with review limited to winners.
  • Review setup and process in progress

Open items

  • Legality of prize pool sharing, specifically the per-player growing pot structure. If the review comes back unfavorable, fallback is sponsor-funded prizes with player fees covering software only.
  • Final entry fee number and the software versus prize pool split
  • Final payout ladder and how positions scale with field size
  • Escrow setup, who administers it, and who reconciles the ledger
  • Remittance deadline date
  • Refund policy if a tour is cancelled before week one
  • Trackman pricing
  • Tiebreaker rules
  • Minimum participation floor (none currently, open to discussion)
  • Registration deadline
  • Sponsorship details
