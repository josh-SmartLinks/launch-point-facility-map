LaunchPoint Global Tour: Initial Proposal

Everything below is a first draft. It is 100% open to feedback and redlining. Nothing here is locked in. Bring changes to the August 15 meeting or send them ahead of time.

Three seasons: Fall Tour, Winter Tour, Spring Tour.

Format

  • Each club selects the courses they want to play, including local courses near them
  • Selections are compiled into a schedule and agreed upon by all clubs before the season starts
  • One course per week
  • Club selections are weighted by participant count. If we go over on submissions, we’ll run a popularity vote to narrow the list.
  • GS Pro on one side, Trackman on the other

Competition Rules

  • Sim settings are locked and identical across all clubs
  • Gross scoring only, open to discussion
  • One scored attempt per week. Round must be declared before it starts and streamed start to finish. That score stands. Unlimited unscored practice rounds allowed.
  • Weekly window runs Monday through Sunday, open to discussion
  • Leaderboard updated live on this site
  • Tiebreaker rules to be discussed

Pricing (GS Pro)

  • $1,000 total, split evenly among participating clubs
  • $5 per tournament player, per month
  • This is at cost. Zero collection on my end. All numbers fully disclosed to participating clubs.
  • Trackman pricing in progress
  • Recommended: collect the full amount from each player at the start of the tour. Option to bundle all three tours for players who want the full season.

Prize Pool

  • Prize pool is funded per player, so the pot grows with every entry. More players, bigger payout. Recommended starting point of $1,000 per tour as a baseline, open to discussion.
  • Payouts are spread across the field, not just the top. Proposed paying positions: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, then 10th, 25th, 50th, and 100th. All paying positions are published before the tour starts. Nothing is randomly drawn.
  • Winners per club, winners per course, and potentially a season-long overall champion
  • Weekly closest to the pin and low round of the week can run alongside the main format
  • Prize packages could include cash or a trip to a premium club (Pebble Beach as an example)
  • Cash prize tax review in progress

On payout placements

Yes to scattered placements, with one rule: publish the paying positions before the tour starts and never draw them randomly.

Predetermined and skill-based (10th place pays because you finished 10th) is fine. Randomly drawing which positions pay turns a skill contest into a chance-based distribution of pooled player money — the worst version of the legality question we’re already trying to answer. Same fun, none of that risk, if the ladder is published up front.

With gross-only scoring, a 15-handicap has zero reason to enter if only the top 3 pay. Give them a realistic target at 25th or 50th and they play all season. That’s the participation problem partly solved.

Suggested ladder, scaling with field size: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, then 10th, 25th, 50th, 100th, plus weekly closest to the pin and a low round of the week.

Meeting note: a pot that grows with every entry makes the structure look more like pooled wagering, not less. Flag it for whoever reviews the legality question.

Sponsorship

  • Actively exploring sponsors for the tour. More details to come.

Integrity

  • All scored rounds must be livestreamed
  • Streams stored on YouTube and linked to each round. Can remain private, with review limited to winners.
  • Review setup and process in progress

Why this works

The $1,000 challenge made the money back in a day and a half and kept bays essentially fully booked with people competing.

Open items

  • Legality of prize pool sharing, including the per-player growing pot structure
  • Final payout ladder and how positions scale with field size
  • Trackman pricing
  • Tiebreaker rules
  • Minimum participation floor (none currently, open to discussion)
  • Registration deadline
  • Sponsorship details
